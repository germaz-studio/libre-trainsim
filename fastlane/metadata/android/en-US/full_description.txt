Libre TrainSim is a free, realistic open-source 3D train driving simulator powered by Godot Engine, ported natively to Android for F-Droid by Oskar Gerlicz-Kowalczuk (Germaz Studio).

Experience train simulation on mobile with complete cab controls, realistic signaling systems, passenger handling, and multiple tutorial scenarios.

Features:
* Realistic train driving physics and responsive touch controls (Mobile HUD)
* German signaling system tutorials (Hp, Vr, Ks, Hl) and safety systems (SiFa, PZB)
* Optimized 3D graphics for mobile devices with safe memory usage (works smoothly on 4 GB RAM)
* Fullscreen immersive mode with customizable view distances
* 100% Free and Open Source (GPL-3.0-or-later)
* Completely offline, zero ads, zero telemetry, zero trackers
